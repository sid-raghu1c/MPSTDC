Frequently asked

How do I book an MP Tourism hotel or resort?

Pick a destination, dates and guests in the search bar, choose your room and pay securely online. Confirmation is sent instantly by email and SMS.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Yes. Sign in and open My Bookings to cancel. Refunds follow the MPSTDC cancellation policy and go back to the original payment method.

Which payment methods are accepted?

All payments run through an SSL-secured payment gateway supporting cards, UPI and net banking.

Where can I see my past bookings?

Your full booking history is in your profile on the website and in the MP Tourism app.
